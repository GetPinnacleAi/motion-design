## Changes from the first cut, with sources

- **AI Employees (scenes 4 and 10):** the pricing page lists the AI Employee only on Growth ($397/mo) and Pro ($497/mo), not Core. Both scenes now say "Growth & Pro plans". The homepage's About paragraph still says every account includes AI employees, so the site disagrees with itself; the video follows the pricing page.
- **Reviews AI (scene 9):** AI-drafted review replies come with Reviews AI, which is listed under Growth and Pro. Added "Reviews AI · Growth & Pro" next to the Approve button.
- **Tech Setup call (scene 11):** "We configure everything with you" became "We set up the technical pieces with you", the pricing page's wording. The automation page says building workflows is not part of setup.
- **AI Employee description (scene 10):** "captures leads" became "qualifies leads", matching "answers, qualifies, and books" on the AI Employee page. "Day or night" and "Online 24/7" stay; the page title says "Answers Leads & Books Jobs 24/7".
- **CTA (scene 13):** "Book your demo" became "Book a live walkthrough". The site's demo is a recorded video; "Book a Live Walkthrough" is the button for booking time with the team.

## Confirmed unchanged

Unlimited users, no contracts, the 9 included features, missed-call text-back, the inbox channels, automatic review requests, approve-before-posting review replies, two recorded 1-on-1 Zoom onboarding calls, and bi-weekly live office hours all match the live site.
